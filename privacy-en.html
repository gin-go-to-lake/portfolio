Privacy

Thank you for visiting Giulia Cornaggia — illustrations' website. We are committed to protecting your privacy and want to inform you about how we collect, use and protect your personal data in accordance with applicable law.

Data Controller Cornaggia Giulia
giuliacornaggia@gmail.com

Collection and use of personal information

Your personal information is used only for the following purposes:

  • To provide and manage the services offered on the site (e.g. responding to commission requests)
  • To comply with legal or contractual obligations
  • To contact clients with whom a working relationship is already in place, including occasional end-of-year greetings
  • To create aggregated, non-identifying statistical data
  • To comply with applicable laws and regulations

Hosting and data storage

The site is hosted on the Netlify platform. The site's contact form is managed via Netlify Forms: messages sent through the form are transmitted to and stored on Netlify's servers before being forwarded to the Owner's email address. Netlify acts as a data processor for this data; for further information, please refer to Netlify's privacy policy.

Cookies and similar technologies

This site does not use profiling cookies, tracking pixels, or traffic analysis tools (e.g. Google Analytics). The only data stored in the browser is the user's chosen language (Italian/English), saved via the browser's local storage — not a cookie in the technical sense — solely to remember the preference on the next visit. This data stays on the user's device and is never transmitted to the Owner.

Should traffic analysis tools or third-party cookies be introduced in the future, this notice will be updated accordingly and, where required by law, an appropriate consent banner will be put in place.

Links to third-party websites

The site may contain links to third-party websites (e.g. Instagram, publishers, partners) that have their own privacy policies. The Owner is not responsible for the practices of such sites and recommends reading their privacy notices carefully.

Communications and contact

The Owner may contact the user to respond to a request, manage an ongoing commission, or send occasional end-of-year greetings to contacts with whom a working relationship already exists. Communications take place by email. Should a contractual relationship arise that involves shipping physical materials (e.g. prints, originals), the data necessary for shipping (name and address) may be shared with the carrier responsible for delivery, solely for that purpose.

What information we collect

We collect the personal information users voluntarily provide, such as name and email address via the contact form, or a shipping address in case of delivery of physical materials. We do not collect browsing data, IP addresses, or session statistics through third-party tools, as none are present on the site.

Withdrawing consent and user rights

Users may withdraw their consent to the collection of personal information at any time, by writing to giuliacornaggia@gmail.com. The request will be processed and the personal data removed from the database, except where retention is required by law.

Users may also contact the Owner at the same address to request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information collected, in accordance with EU Regulation 2016/679 (GDPR). The Owner undertakes to respond as quickly as possible.

Updates to this notice

The Owner reserves the right to modify this notice at any time. Changes take effect upon publication on the site. Users are encouraged to check this page periodically for updates.

For any questions about this notice or the processing of your personal data, please write to giuliacornaggia@gmail.com.
